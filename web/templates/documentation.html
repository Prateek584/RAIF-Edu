System Guide & Architecture

How the Responsible AI Framework Works

This framework acts as a smart guardrail between students, teachers, and AI models. Whenever a question or answer passes through, it checks 6 important areas to make sure learning is safe, fair, and effective.

1. Student Privacy Shield

Hides student names, school IDs, email addresses, and phone numbers before the question leaves the school network. Also checks parental consent for learners under 13 (under FERPA & COPPA rules).

2. Fair Grading for Everyone

Audits grading algorithms to make sure every student group passes at equal rates. Uses the famous 80% Fairness Rule and checks teacher comments to prevent stereotyping.

3. Safety & Kindness Guard

Instantly detects cyberbullying, mean words, self-harm, and inappropriate adult topics. Also measures word difficulty so answers match the student's grade level.

4. Smart Tutor (Anti-Cheating)

Stops students from using AI as a shortcut to write their essays or solve homework. Guides the AI to give hints, ask reflective questions, and encourage real learning.

5. Textbook Fact Checker

Compares the AI's scientific, mathematical, and historical statements against verified textbook lessons to catch false or made-up facts (hallucinations).

6. Clear Scoring & Teacher Check

Explains grades clearly so students and parents know exactly why a grade was given. Automatically sends any risky or flagged answers to a real teacher.

Mathematical & Evaluation Formulas

Disparate Impact Ratio (DIR)

Compares the passing rate of unprivileged students to privileged students:

DIR = PassRate(Unprivileged) / PassRate(Privileged)

Must be at least 0.80 to satisfy the US EEOC 80% Four-Fifths Rule.

Demographic Parity Difference (DPD)

The absolute difference in favorable outcome probability:

DPD = | PassRate(Group A) - PassRate(Group B) |

Target is close to 0.0 (less than 10% gap).

Flesch-Kincaid Reading Grade Level

Calculates the school grade level required to understand the text:

FKGL = 0.39*(words/sent) + 11.8*(syllables/words) - 15.59

Ensures learning explanations match student comprehension levels.

Overall Responsible AI Score

Weighted average of all 5 safety and learning pillars:

Score = 25% Safety + 25% Privacy + 25% Learning + 15% Facts + 10% Rubric

Score of 75+ is Approved; below 65 triggers Teacher Review.
